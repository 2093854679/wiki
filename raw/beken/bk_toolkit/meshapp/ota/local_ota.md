# 7.1. local ota

Local ota从手机上获取对应的ota文件。

选择ota方式

[![file_tree](../../../_assets/2283ec050ae80aa97c6c-local_ota_type.jpg)](../../../_assets/2283ec050ae80aa97c6c-local_ota_type.jpg)

点击按钮，可将服务器上最新的版本文件，下载到手机上。选择要使用的ota文件。

[![file_tree](../../../_assets/c6cb88f4a630e3ff3d65-local_ota_select_file_1.jpg)](../../../_assets/c6cb88f4a630e3ff3d65-local_ota_select_file_1.jpg)

[![file_tree](../../../_assets/9a81fabb43eab88a6922-local_ota_select_file_2.jpg)](../../../_assets/9a81fabb43eab88a6922-local_ota_select_file_2.jpg)

使用当前手机，开启手机热点（必须是2.4G），然后填写热点名称和密码。

[![file_tree](../../../_assets/aaf8c91fef48e8b6fc78-local_ota_hot.jpg)](../../../_assets/aaf8c91fef48e8b6fc78-local_ota_hot.jpg)

[![file_tree](../../../_assets/a4d145b0891d8e55d42f-local_ota_hot2.jpg)](../../../_assets/a4d145b0891d8e55d42f-local_ota_hot2.jpg)

开始ota。

[![file_tree](../../../_assets/25297d40565e6f411ac4-ota_start.jpg)](../../../_assets/25297d40565e6f411ac4-ota_start.jpg)

ota结束，会检测目标版本与节点版本是否一致，并提示结果。

[![file_tree](../../../_assets/5fdc4e8e3956a9cfb0e5-ota_end.jpg)](../../../_assets/5fdc4e8e3956a9cfb0e5-ota_end.jpg)
