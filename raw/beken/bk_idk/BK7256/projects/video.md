## 芯片功能

|  | RGB | I8080 | DMA2D | DVP | UVC | JPEG | QSPI(LCD) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BK7256 | Y | Y | Y | Y | Y | Y | Y |

- 1x LCD interface (16-bit parallel RGB and I8080)
